# Title: Counterfactual Entropy Temperatures for Context-Sensitive Character Language Models
# Experiment description: Extend GPTConfig/GPT with use_counterfactual_temperature, log_temp_max, temp_gate_init, base_anchor_weight, and entropy_target_weight. Add a small temperature_head Linear(n_embd, 1) and a learned scalar gate initialized so the model starts as the ordinary baseline. In GPT.forward, compute ordinary tied lm_head logits, predict a bounded per-token log temperature from final hidden states, and divide logits by exp(log_temp) before the calibrated cross-entropy; also expose a diagnostic path with temperature disabled. Train with calibrated CE plus a small base-logit CE anchor on every iteration. After warmup_iters and every temperature_interval iterations, preserve a recent suffix such as the final 96 or 128 tokens, replace the older prefix with a rolled same-batch prefix, and run the counterfactual view under torch.no_grad() with temperature disabled. On the protected suffix, form a detached teacher distribution by averaging clean base probabilities and counterfactual base probabilities, then compute its entropy. Add a small ramped Huber loss matching the entropy of the calibrated clean logits to this counterfactual-mixture entropy, while low-disagreement positions naturally stay near the base entropy. Extend estimate_loss to report calibrated validation loss, base validation loss, expected calibration error, Brier score, mean temperature, entropy-target error, validation loss by counterfactual-disagreement bucket, and held-out prefix-counterfactual robustness. Compare against the baseline, learned global temperature, unsupervised adaptive temperature, and a shuffled-counterfactual-target control across shakespeare_char, enwik8, and text8. Generation uses the learned one-pass calibrated logits and requires no counterfactual views.
