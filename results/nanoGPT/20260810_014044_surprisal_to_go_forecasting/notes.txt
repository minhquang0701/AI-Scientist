# Title: Surprisal-to-Go Forecasting for Uncertainty-Aware Autoregressive Representations
# Experiment description: Add a training-only auxiliary objective that asks each hidden state to predict detached future average surprisal over several horizons. Extend GPTConfig with use_surprisal_forecasting, surprisal_windows, surprisal_loss_weight, and surprisal_ramp_iters. In GPT.__init__, add a small LayerNorm plus Linear(n_embd, len(surprisal_windows)) regression head. Modify GPT.forward to optionally return final hidden states and per-token next-character cross-entropy losses using reduction='none', while preserving the ordinary logits, mean loss, validation, generation, and final reporting behavior. In the training loop, compute the standard next-token cross-entropy as the primary loss. After warmup_iters, use the detached per-token NLL from the same clean forward pass to build strictly future targets: for each position t and horizon W, predict the average NLL over positions t+1 through t+W, masking invalid tail positions. Use cumulative sums for efficient target construction, normalize targets with detached running mean and standard deviation per horizon, and train the auxiliary head with masked Huber loss. Optimize total_loss = next_token_loss + alpha_t * surprisal_forecast_loss, ramping alpha_t to surprisal_loss_weight. Keep estimate_loss, best validation loss, inference speed, and generation based only on the ordinary lm_head. Add diagnostics for validation correlation and R^2 between predicted and realized future surprisal, validation NLL stratified by predicted future difficulty, calibration by confidence bucket, and generated entropy or repetition metrics. Compare against the baseline using surprisal_windows=[4,16,64] with surprisal_loss_weight values such as 0.01, 0.03, and 0.1, plus a single-horizon ablation, across shakespeare_char, enwik8, and text8.
