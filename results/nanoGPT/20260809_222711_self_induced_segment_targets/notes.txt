Store spans in length-descending lookup dictionaries for greedy longest-match assignment. Modify get_batch to sample block_size + max_segment_len tokens internally, return the usual X and Y, and optionally return segment_targets where each position is the longest lexicon segment beginning at the corresponding next-character target position. Validation, generation, final_info, and inference speed remain based on the ordinary character lm_head. Add diagnostics for segment prediction accuracy, multi-character span coverage, average matched span length, and auxiliary parameter overhead. Compare the baseline against K values such as 256, 1024, and 4096, plus ablations with max_segment_len 4 versus 6, across shakespeare_char, enwik8, and text8.
