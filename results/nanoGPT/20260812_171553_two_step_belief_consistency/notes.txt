# Title: Two-Step Belief Consistency for Autoregressive Character Models
# Experiment description: Modify GPT.forward() to optionally return final hidden states after ln_f. Add one lightweight horizon-2 belief head Linear(n_embd, vocab_size), used only during training and diagnostics; ordinary evaluation and generation continue to use the tied lm_head. Train every iteration with the standard next-character cross-entropy. After warmup_iters, every belief_interval iterations, subsample a modest number of valid anchor positions t < block_size - 1. For each anchor, take the detached next-character distribution from the ordinary lm_head at position t, keep the top_k candidates, create branched copies where X[:, t + 1] is replaced by each candidate, run one extra batched forward pass, and collect detached one-step distributions at position t + 1. Form a normalized top-k approximation to the model-implied two-step predictive distribution by marginalizing over candidate next characters. Add a small ramped KL loss from this detached branched two-step distribution to the horizon-2 belief-head distribution at h_t, optionally anchored by a weak hard horizon-2 cross-entropy to Y[:, t + 1]. Extend estimate_loss() to report ordinary train/validation loss as the primary metric, horizon-2 NLL, branch-consistency KL, top-k branch coverage, expected calibration error, train-val gap, total train time, and unchanged generation tokens per second. Compare the baseline, hard horizon-2 head only, two-step belief consistency, and a shuffled-branch-target control across shakespeare_char, enwik8, and text8.
