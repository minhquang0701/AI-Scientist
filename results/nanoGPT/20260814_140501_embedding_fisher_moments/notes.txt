# Title: Embedding-Fisher Moment Calibration for Tied-Softmax Language Models
# Experiment description: Keep the GPT architecture, data loader, optimizer, evaluation path, and generation code unchanged. Add an embedding_fisher_moment_loss(logits, targets, embedding_weight, num_bins=5) helper. For valid target positions, compute p = softmax(logits), detach the tied output embedding matrix W, form mu = p @ W, and compute the model-predicted embedding Fisher energy pred_energy = sum_i p_i ||W_i||^2 - ||mu||^2. Compute the realized target energy real_energy = ||W_target - mu.detach()||^2. Bucket valid positions by detached pred_energy quantiles, and for buckets with enough examples penalize a Huber or squared gap between mean(pred_energy) and mean(real_energy.detach()), normalized by the detached bucket mean energy for scale stability. Train every iteration with ordinary next-character cross-entropy; after warmup_iters and every fisher_interval iterations, add lambda_fisher times this loss, ramping lambda_fisher to small values such as 0.005, 0.01, or 0.05. Extend estimate_loss to report ordinary train and validation NLL as the primary metric, embedding-Fisher calibration gap by bucket, expected calibration error, Brier score, rare-token validation loss, repeated versus novel target loss, train-val gap, total train time, and unchanged generation tokens per second. Compare against the CE baseline, entropy-only typicality calibration, and a shuffled-bin or random-bucket Fisher calibration control across shakespeare_char, enwik8, and text8.
