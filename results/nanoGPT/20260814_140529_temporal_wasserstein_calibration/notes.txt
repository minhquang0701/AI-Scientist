# Title: Temporal Wasserstein Calibration for Sequence-Aware Character Language Models
# Experiment description: Keep the GPT architecture, data loader, optimizer, ordinary next-character cross-entropy objective, evaluation path, and generation code unchanged. Add a temporal_wasserstein_loss(logits, targets, chunk_len=32 or 64, huber_delta=0.05) helper. For valid target positions inside non-overlapping chunks, compute predicted probabilities from logits and compare them to the empirical one-hot target sequence by taking cumulative sums over time for each vocabulary token. Penalize the Huber or squared difference between predicted and empirical cumulative token mass, normalized by chunk length and vocabulary size; also log the endpoint-only marginal error separately so timing error can be distinguished from simple chunk-frequency mismatch. Train with ordinary CE on every iteration and, after warmup_iters every wasserstein_interval steps, add lambda_wasserstein * temporal_wasserstein_loss with lambda ramped conservatively to 0.005-0.05. Extend estimate_loss to report ordinary train/validation NLL as the primary metric, temporal Wasserstein calibration error, endpoint marginal error, expected calibration error, validation loss by confidence bucket, generated character-frequency KL, repeated n-gram rate, distinct n-gram fraction, train-val gap, total train time, and unchanged inference tokens per second. Compare against the CE-only baseline, local marginal calibration, temporal spectral calibration, and a shuffled-time target control across shakespeare_char, enwik8, and text8.
