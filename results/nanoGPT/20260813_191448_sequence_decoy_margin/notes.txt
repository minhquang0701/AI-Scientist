# Title: Sequence-Decoy Margin Regularization for Memorization-Resistant Character Language Models
# Experiment description: Add a controlled memorization audit and a generic sequence-level regularizer to train(). Before training each dataset, generate synthetic canary strings from characters in meta.pkl, verify that they do not occur in train.bin or val.bin, and inject them into training with fixed repeat counts such as 1, 5, and 20 for auditing. The baseline trains on this canary-augmented stream with ordinary next-character cross-entropy. For the proposed method, keep the same CE objective, but after warmup_iters and every decoy_margin_interval, take a small subset of ordinary training windows, split each into a prefix and continuation, build matched decoy continuations by batch permutation or character-frequency-matched shuffling, score true and decoy continuation log likelihoods under the same prefix, and add a small ramped penalty relu((ll_true - logmeanexp(ll_decoys)) / continuation_len - margin)^2. Do not treat canary windows specially during regularization. Evaluation reports ordinary validation loss, canary exposure, true-continuation rank among decoys, extraction success from canary prefixes, metrics by canary repeat count, train-val gap, total train time, and unchanged generation tokens per second. Compare against CE-only, uniform label smoothing, confidence-cap regularization, and shuffled-decoy controls across shakespeare_char, enwik8, and text8.
