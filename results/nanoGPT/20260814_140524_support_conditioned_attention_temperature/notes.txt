Multiply q by inv_temp before scaled_dot_product_attention, preserving flash attention and leaving the disabled path unchanged. Optionally add a tiny L2 penalty on raw_alpha after warmup. Compare the baseline, a zero-coefficient enabled control, a learned per-head constant-temperature control, fixed analytic support scaling, and learned per-head support-conditioned scaling across shakespeare_char, enwik8, and text8.
