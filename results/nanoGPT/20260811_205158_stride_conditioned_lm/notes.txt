Add the auxiliary losses with a ramped total weight lambda_stride up to 0.1 or 0.25. Extend estimate_loss to report ordinary validation loss as the primary metric plus stride-2 and stride-4 compressed-time validation losses. Keep sampling unchanged by always using stride_id=0. Compare against the baseline and a control without stride embeddings across shakespeare_char, enwik8, and text8 using best ordinary validation loss, strided-view validation loss, convergence speed, train-val gap, total training time, and unchanged inference tokens per second.
