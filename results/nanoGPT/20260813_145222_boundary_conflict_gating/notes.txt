# Title: Boundary-Conflict Gated Training for Crop-Robust Autoregressive Language Modeling
# Experiment description: Modify GPT.forward to optionally return final hidden states after ln_f. In the training loop, compute unreduced next-character cross-entropy and split target positions into low-context boundary tokens, such as the first 32 or 64 positions, and higher-context interior tokens. After warmup_iters, estimate boundary-interior gradient agreement from the same forward pass using detached output-layer gradient sketches: for each region, average outer-product sketches between hidden states and softmax(logits) - one_hot(target), optionally projected with a fixed random matrix for speed. Convert the cosine agreement into a bounded boundary weight, using full boundary weight when agreement is positive and reducing toward a floor such as 0.25 when agreement is negative, with EMA smoothing for stability. Optimize the weighted token loss, normalized by effective token weight. Compare against the original uniform loss, fixed context-ramp weighting, boundary-drop weighting, and a random-position split control. Extend estimate_loss to report ordinary validation loss, interior-suffix validation loss, loss by absolute position bucket, boundary-interior gradient agreement, active boundary weight, train-val gap, total train time, and unchanged generation tokens per second across shakespeare_char, enwik8, and text8.
