# Title: Agreement-Gated Suffix Barycenter Consistency for Prefix-Robust Language Modeling
# Experiment description: Keep the GPT architecture, data loader, ordinary next-character cross-entropy objective, evaluation path, and generation code unchanged. Add a helper that constructs prefix-replaced views of a batch by preserving a protected recent suffix of length S, such as 96 or 128 tokens, while replacing the older prefix with prefixes from rolled batch elements. During training, always compute the standard clean next-token CE loss. After warmup_iters, every consistency_interval iterations, run the model on the clean batch and one or two prefix-replaced views. Align logits on the shared suffix after a burn-in of 16 or 32 positions. Compute detached temperature-scaled probabilities for each view, form their barycenter distribution, and add a small symmetric KL loss from each view to the barycenter. Downweight each suffix position with a detached agreement gate based on the views' Jensen-Shannon disagreement, so strongly prefix-sensitive positions contribute less. Ramp lambda_suffix up to 0.05 or 0.1. Extend estimate_loss to report ordinary train and validation loss, prefix-replaced validation loss, clean-to-replaced degradation, suffix JS disagreement, gated versus ungated consistency diagnostics, loss by distance into the suffix, train-val gap, total train time, and unchanged generation tokens per second. Compare against the baseline, ungated suffix barycenter consistency, clean-teacher-to-replaced-view KL, and replaced-view CE-only controls across shakespeare_char, enwik8, and text8.
