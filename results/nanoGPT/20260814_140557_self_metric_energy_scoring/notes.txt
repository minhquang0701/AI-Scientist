# Title: Self-Metric Energy Scoring for Calibrated Character Language Models
# Experiment description: Keep the GPT architecture, data loader, optimizer, evaluation path, and generation code unchanged. Add a self_metric_energy_loss(logits, targets, embedding_weight) helper. After warmup_iters and every energy_interval iterations, detach and L2-normalize the tied token/output embedding matrix, compute a vocab-by-vocab Euclidean distance matrix, and compute the categorical energy score over valid targets: E_p[d(z, y)] - 0.5 * E_{p,p}[d(z, z')], where p = softmax(logits). Train with ordinary next-character cross-entropy as the primary loss plus a small ramped lambda_energy term, sweeping conservative values such as 0.01, 0.02, and 0.05. Because the distance matrix is detached, the auxiliary shapes predictive distributions but cannot collapse the embedding metric. Compare against the CE baseline, a fixed random-metric energy score, a row-shuffled learned-metric control, and identity-kernel Brier regularization. Extend estimate_loss to report ordinary train/validation NLL as the primary metric, energy score, expected calibration error, Brier score, rare versus common token loss, repeated versus novel target loss, train-val gap, total train time, and unchanged generation tokens per second across shakespeare_char, enwik8, and text8.
