# Title: Crop-State Marginal Distillation for Boundary-Robust Character Language Modeling
# Experiment description: Keep the default GPT training, evaluation, and generation path unchanged. Extend GPTConfig with train_seq_len, use_crop_state_marginalization, num_crop_states, num_crop_state_tokens, crop_burnin_len, crop_band_len, crop_state_gate_init, crop_interval, and lambda_crop_max. Add learned crop-state prefix embeddings of shape num_crop_states by num_crop_state_tokens by n_embd, a small prefix-position embedding table, and a CropStateEncoder that embeds and mean-pools only X[:, :crop_burnin_len] to predict log probabilities over crop states. Modify GPT.forward to optionally prepend one selected crop-state prefix before the real tokens, while real tokens keep their original position ids and prefix logits are discarded. In train(), always compute ordinary no-prefix next-character CE. After warmup_iters and every crop_interval iterations, take a scheduled subbatch, expand it over all crop states, run the prefixed forward pass, and score only post-burn-in boundary positions crop_burnin_len through crop_burnin_len + crop_band_len - 1. Combine encoder log probabilities and state-conditioned target log probabilities with logsumexp to form a crop-state marginal NLL. Also form a detached prior-weighted marginal predictive distribution over crop states for the same positions and add a small KL distillation term from this marginal distribution to the ordinary no-prefix logits, so the deployed path benefits from the latent crop-state teacher. Ramp the combined auxiliary weight while keeping ordinary CE dominant. Extend estimate_loss to report ordinary validation loss, post-burn-in boundary-band loss, interior loss, crop-marginal validation loss, no-prefix-to-marginal KL, boundary-to-interior loss gap, crop-state posterior entropy, state usage, train-val gap, total train time, and ordinary generation tokens per second. Compare the baseline, unconditional learned crop states, deterministic burn-in virtual prefixes, crop-state marginalization without distillation, crop-state marginal distillation, and a shuffled-burn-in encoder control across shakespeare_char, enwik8, and text8.
