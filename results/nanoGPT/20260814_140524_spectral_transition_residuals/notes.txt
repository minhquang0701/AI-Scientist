# Experiment description: Before training each dataset, scan train.bin once to compute smoothed predecessor distributions, successor distributions, and token frequencies for every character. Extend GPTConfig with use_spectral_transition_residuals, graph_basis_dim, graph_gate_init, base_anchor_weight, and residual_l2. In GPT.__init__, register the spectral basis as a buffer, add a coefficient head Linear(n_embd, graph_basis_dim), and add a learned scalar gate initialized to zero. Replace direct lm_head calls with logits_from_hidden: compute ordinary tied-softmax logits, predict context-dependent spectral coefficients, form centered residual logits coeffs @ basis.T, and add the gated residual to the base logits. Train with next-character cross-entropy on the corrected logits plus a small base-logit anchor loss so the ordinary tied head remains useful. Modify estimate_loss and generate to use logits_from_hidden, while also logging validation loss with the residual disabled. Compare the baseline, spectral transition residuals with basis dimensions 4/8/16, a spectrum-preserving shuffled-basis control, and a matched unconstrained low-rank logit residual across shakespeare_char, enwik8, and text8. Report corrected validation loss, base-only validation loss, rare versus common token loss, repeated versus novel target loss, expected calibration error, learned gate value, spectral energy by mode, parameter overhead, total train time, and inference tokens per second.
