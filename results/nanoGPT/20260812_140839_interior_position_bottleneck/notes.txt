# Title: Interior Position-Information Bottlenecks for Origin-Robust Character Transformers
# Experiment description: Modify GPT.forward() to optionally return final hidden states after ln_f and optionally accept position_ids, defaulting to the existing arange positions so standard training, evaluation, and generation are unchanged. Add a small train-only position_adversary head that predicts coarse absolute-position buckets from hidden states through a gradient-reversal layer. During training, compute ordinary next-character cross-entropy on every iteration. After warmup_iters, add a weak ramped adversarial position loss with maximum weight 0.01 to 0.05, using 16 or 32 coarse buckets and applying it only to interior positions after a burn-in such as the first 32 tokens, where absolute position mostly reflects missing left context. The adversary learns to predict position, while the LM hidden states receive reversed gradients, discouraging unnecessary crop-origin information. Extend estimate_loss() to report ordinary train/validation loss, validation loss by absolute-position bucket, detached position-probe accuracy, short-context validation losses at 32/64/128/256, and shifted-position validation loss when a larger positional table such as 512 is enabled. Compare the baseline, interior position bottleneck, random-position-offset training, and bottleneck plus random offsets across shakespeare_char, enwik8, and text8. Report best validation loss, train-val gap, position leakage, shifted-origin degradation, total train time, and unchanged generation tokens per second.
