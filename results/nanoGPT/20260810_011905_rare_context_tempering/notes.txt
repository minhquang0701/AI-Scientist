Modify GPT.forward or the training loop to compute per-token cross-entropy with reduction='none' and log probabilities. After warmup_iters, convert support counts into a per-position smoothing value epsilon_t = epsilon_max * max(0, support_threshold - count) / support_threshold, so rare contexts receive stronger smoothing and frequent contexts remain nearly baseline.
