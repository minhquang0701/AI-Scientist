# Title: Prequential Innovation Sketching for Evidence-Aware Character Language Models
# Experiment description: Modify GPT.forward() to optionally return final hidden states after ln_f in addition to logits and loss. Add a small training-only innovation_head Linear(n_embd, sketch_dim), and create a fixed zero-mean normalized random projection matrix R of shape vocab_size by sketch_dim, with sketch_dim such as 16 or 32. Train every iteration with ordinary next-character cross-entropy as the primary objective. After warmup_iters and every innovation_interval iterations, compute detached probabilities p = softmax(logits). For positions t > 0, define a leakage-free prequential innovation target from the token that has just been observed: innovation_t = R[X[:, t]] - p[:, t - 1, :] @ R. Train innovation_head(hidden[:, t, :]) to predict this detached sketch using Huber or MSE loss, optionally clipping or RMS-normalizing targets for stability, with lambda_innovation ramped to 0.02 to 0.10. Evaluation and generation ignore the auxiliary head and use the ordinary lm_head unchanged. Extend estimate_loss() to report ordinary train/validation loss as the primary metric, innovation prediction MSE or cosine similarity, expected calibration error, validation loss by predicted-innovation-magnitude bucket, repeated-target versus novel-target loss, residual autocorrelation diagnostics, train-val gap, total train time, and unchanged inference tokens per second. Compare against the baseline, an observed-token-sketch auxiliary without subtracting the prior forecast, a scalar surprisal-prediction auxiliary, a shuffled-innovation-label control, and a detached-hidden auxiliary-probe control across shakespeare_char, enwik8, and text8.
