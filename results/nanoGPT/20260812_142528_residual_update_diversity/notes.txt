After warmup_iters, every diversity_interval iterations such as 4 or 8, run the normal forward pass with residual updates returned, sample at most 1024 valid batch-time positions, flatten each layer's sampled residual updates into a matrix, center it across sampled positions, L2-normalize the flattened matrix, and add a penalty equal to the mean squared cosine similarity between different layers' residual-update matrices. Evaluation and generation use the unchanged ordinary forward path. Extend estimate_loss to report ordinary train and validation loss, residual-update similarity, per-layer update norms, effective update rank, train-val gap, total train time, and inference tokens per second.
