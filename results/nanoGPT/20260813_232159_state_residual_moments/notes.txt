# Title: State-Conditional Residual Moment Matching for Calibrated Character Language Models
# Experiment description: Modify GPT.forward() to optionally return final hidden states after ln_f. After loading each dataset vocabulary, create a fixed normalized random projection matrix R of shape vocab_size by residual_dim, with residual_dim such as 32 or 64. Add a small train-only ResidualMomentCritic MLP that maps hidden states to projected forecast residual sketches. On scheduled auxiliary iterations after warmup_iters, run the normal forward pass with return_hidden=True, compute probabilities p = softmax(logits), and form residual sketches e_t = R[target_t] - p_t @ R for valid targets. Train the critic with MSE to predict e_t.detach() from hidden.detach(). Update the GPT with ordinary next-character cross-entropy plus a small nonnegative moment penalty: norm(mean(clip(critic(hidden).detach()) * e_t over valid positions))^2, with lambda_moment ramped conservatively to values such as 0.005, 0.01, and 0.02. Keep evaluation and generation unchanged and ignore the critic at inference. Extend estimate_loss() to report ordinary train/validation NLL, expected calibration error, Brier score, held-out residual-prediction MSE or R2, residual moment penalty, validation loss by confidence bucket, train-val gap, total train time, and unchanged generation tokens per second. Compare against the baseline, uniform label smoothing, a fixed random hidden-residual covariance penalty, and a shuffled-residual-target critic control across shakespeare_char, enwik8, and text8.
