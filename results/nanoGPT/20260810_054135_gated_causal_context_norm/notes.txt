Support either full-prefix statistics or a fixed recent window using cumulative sums, with a variance floor for early-token stability. In Block.__init__, add an optional LayerNorm, the CausalContextNorm module, a Linear projection back to n_embd, dropout, and a learnable scalar gate initialized to a small value such as 0.01. Keep the standard next-character cross-entropy objective, optimizer, validation, generation, and final reporting unchanged. Compare the baseline against causal context normalization in all layers and in only the lower half of layers, using full-prefix statistics and a fixed window such as 128. Report best validation loss, convergence speed, total training time, inference tokens per second, generated samples, parameter overhead, learned gate values, activation variance diagnostics, and cropped-context validation losses at 64 and 128 tokens across shakespeare_char, enwik8, and text8.
