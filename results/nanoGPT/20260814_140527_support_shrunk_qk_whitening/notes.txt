# Title: Support-Shrunk Causal QK Whitening for Locally Stable Attention
# Experiment description: Extend GPTConfig with use_qk_whitening, qk_whiten_window, qk_whiten_eps, qk_whiten_gate_max, qk_whiten_min_count, detach_qk_stats, and qk_whiten_l2. In CausalSelfAttention.__init__, add bounded learned per-head gates for query and key whitening, initialized to zero so the enabled model starts exactly as the baseline. In CausalSelfAttention.forward, after q and k are reshaped, compute inclusive causal rolling means and variances over the most recent qk_whiten_window visible positions for each batch, head, and feature using cumulative sums. Use support shrinkage so positions with fewer than qk_whiten_min_count visible tokens interpolate the rolling statistics toward zero mean and unit variance, and optionally detach the statistics for stability. Form q_white and k_white, then use q_eff = q + gate_q * (q_white - q) and k_eff = k + gate_k * (k_white - k), leaving v unchanged and feeding q_eff and k_eff into the existing flash or manual attention path. Train with the ordinary next-character cross-entropy objective, optimizer, data loader, evaluation loop, and generation procedure unchanged. Compare the baseline, key-only whitening, query-and-key whitening, centering-only normalization, and a shuffled-statistics control across shakespeare_char, enwik8, and text8. Report ordinary train and validation loss, best validation loss, validation loss at context lengths 64, 128, and 256, repeated-target versus novel-target loss, attention entropy, learned whitening gates by layer and head, q/k scale diagnostics, train-val gap, total train time, and inference tokens per second.
