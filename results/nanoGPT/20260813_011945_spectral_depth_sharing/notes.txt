# Title: Spectral Depth Sharing for Parameter-Efficient Character Transformers
# Experiment description: Add an optional spectral depth-sharing mode for transformer projection weights. Extend GPTConfig with use_spectral_depth_sharing, depth_basis_dim, and depth_basis_type. Precompute fixed orthonormal DCT coefficients over the n_layer depth indices. For each large projection type in the transformer stack, namely attention c_attn, attention c_proj, MLP c_fc, and MLP c_proj, store a small learned bank of basis weight tensors and generate layer l's effective weight as the coefficient-weighted sum of those basis tensors, applying it with F.linear. Keep LayerNorms independent per layer, keep positional and token embeddings unchanged, and leave the default GPT path untouched. With depth_basis_dim=1 this becomes fully shared projection weights across depth; with depth_basis_dim=n_layer it can represent independent layer weights. Compare the original independent 6-layer baseline, basis sizes 1, 2, 3, and 4, a full-basis control, and parameter-matched narrower independent models across shakespeare_char, enwik8, and text8. Keep the data loader, optimizer, ordinary next-character cross-entropy objective, evaluation, sampling, and generation procedure unchanged. Report parameter count, best validation loss, validation loss per million parameters, final train loss, train-val gap, convergence speed, total train time, inference tokens per second, effective layer-to-layer weight similarity, and learned energy by depth basis component.
