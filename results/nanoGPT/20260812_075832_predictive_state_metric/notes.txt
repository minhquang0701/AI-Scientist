# Title: Predictive-State Metric Regularization for Autoregressive Language Models
# Experiment description: Modify GPT.forward to optionally return final hidden states after ln_f in addition to logits and loss. Add a predictive_state_metric_loss(hidden, logits, targets, max_positions=192, pred_temperature=2.0, hidden_temperature=0.1) helper. On auxiliary iterations, flatten valid batch-time positions, optionally discard the highest-entropy predictive positions, randomly subsample at most max_positions, compute detached predictive distributions from logits / pred_temperature, form pairwise predictive similarities using negative Jensen-Shannon distance, and convert each row into a soft neighborhood distribution with the diagonal masked. Normalize the corresponding hidden states, compute pairwise cosine similarities divided by hidden_temperature, and minimize row-wise KL divergence from predictive neighborhoods to hidden-state neighborhoods. In the training loop, keep ordinary next-token cross-entropy on every iteration; after warmup_iters, every metric_interval iterations such as 4 or 8, add a ramped lambda_metric times this loss, with lambda_metric values such as 0.005, 0.01, and 0.02. Evaluation and generation remain unchanged. Extend estimate_loss to report ordinary train and validation loss as the primary metric, plus predictive-state metric loss, correlation between hidden cosine similarity and predictive similarity, expected calibration error, and validation loss by predictive-entropy bucket. Compare against the baseline, an exact-target supervised contrastive control, and a shuffled-predictive-neighborhood control across shakespeare_char, enwik8, and text8.
