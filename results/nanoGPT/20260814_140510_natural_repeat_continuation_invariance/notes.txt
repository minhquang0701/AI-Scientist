# Title: Natural Repeat Continuation Invariance for Prefix-Robust Character Language Modeling
# Experiment description: Before training each dataset, scan train.bin with rolling hashes to build capped indices of naturally repeated exact substrings of length S + 1 for S in {16, 32, 64}, storing only non-overlapping occurrences with distinct preceding contexts. Modify get_batch() to optionally sample paired windows whose final S input tokens and aligned S next-token targets are identical because they come from the same repeated S + 1 substring, while their older prefixes differ. Train with ordinary next-character cross-entropy on normal batches. After warmup_iters, every repeat_invariance_interval iterations, run a paired repeated-substring batch and add a small ramped symmetric temperature-2 KL loss between the two predictive distributions on the shared continuation after a short burn-in, gated down for high-entropy or high-disagreement positions. Keep architecture, default evaluation, and generation unchanged. Extend estimate_loss to report ordinary validation loss, paired-repeat KL on repeated substrings from val.bin, loss by distance into the repeated substring, repeat-pair coverage, train-val gap, total train time, and unchanged generation tokens per second. Compare against the baseline, random paired substrings with matched lengths, synthetic prefix-replacement consistency, and fixed short-span-only ablations across shakespeare_char, enwik8, and text8.
