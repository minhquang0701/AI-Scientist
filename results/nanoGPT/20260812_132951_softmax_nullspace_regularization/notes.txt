# Title: Softmax-Nullspace Regularization for Tied-Embedding Character Transformers
# Experiment description: Modify GPT.forward() to optionally return final hidden states after ln_f. Add a softmax_nullspace_penalty(hidden, embedding_weight) helper that centers the tied output embedding matrix by subtracting its vocabulary mean, computes a detached orthonormal basis for the centered row space using SVD or QR, projects hidden states onto this probability-relevant subspace, and penalizes the residual softmax-null energy normalized by total hidden energy. Recompute the detached basis only on nullspace-regularization iterations to keep overhead small. In the training loop, keep ordinary next-character cross-entropy as the primary objective on every iteration; after warmup_iters, every nullspace_interval iterations, add lambda_null times this penalty with lambda_null ramped to values such as 0.001, 0.005, and 0.01. Include a matched random-subspace control and a simple hidden-norm penalty control to test whether gains come from tied-softmax geometry rather than generic shrinkage. Evaluation and generation remain unchanged. Extend estimate_loss() to report ordinary train and validation loss, softmax-null energy, row-space energy, train-val gap, expected calibration error, total train time, and inference tokens per second. Compare against the baseline across shakespeare_char, enwik8, and text8, using ordinary validation loss as the primary metric and nullspace energy plus calibration as diagnostics.
