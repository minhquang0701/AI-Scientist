Ramp a weak domain-confusion weight after warmup_iters, with lambda_domain values such as 0.01, 0.03, and 0.1, while keeping next-character loss dominant. Compare separate per-dataset models, naive pooled union-vocabulary training, dataset-conditioned pooled training, and shared-character domain-invariant training.
