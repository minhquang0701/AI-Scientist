Extend GPTConfig and GPT with an optional two-entry view embedding, using view_id=0 for ordinary left-to-right modeling and view_id=1 for the suffix-order auxiliary view. Train every iteration with the standard raw next-character cross-entropy. After warmup_iters, every bwt_interval iterations such as 8, build X_bwt and Y_bwt by shifting the BWT token stream, run model(X_bwt, Y_bwt, view_id=1), and add a small ramped auxiliary loss with lambda_bwt in the range 0.05 to 0.15. Keep validation, sampling, and primary reporting on the raw left-to-right view only, with BWT validation used only as a diagnostic. Compare the baseline, a compute-matched extra raw-CE control, a fixed random-permutation auxiliary control, and the BWT suffix-order auxiliary model across shakespeare_char, enwik8, and text8. Report ordinary validation loss, BWT-view loss, validation loss on repeated-substring or high-LZ-match positions, train-val gap, generation repetition and distinct n-gram diagnostics, total train time, and unchanged raw generation tokens per second.
