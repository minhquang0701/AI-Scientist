# Title: Repeat-Frequency Group-DRO for Robust Character Language Modeling
# Experiment description: Keep the GPT architecture, data loader, generation code, and default evaluation unchanged. Before training each dataset, scan train.bin once to assign each token id to one of three frequency buckets: rare, medium, or common. Add compute_repeat_frequency_groups(X, Y, token_freq_bucket), assigning each supervised token to one of nine causal groups from frequency bucket crossed with prefix status: target not seen in the visible prefix, target seen recently within a threshold such as 32 characters, or target seen only at longer distance. In the training loop, compute unreduced per-token cross-entropy from logits. Maintain EMA losses for the nine groups, update them only when a group has enough examples in the batch, and after warmup_iters form capped softmax Group-DRO weights mixed with a small uniform component for stability. Optimize a ramped mixture of ordinary mean CE and weighted group loss, with lambda_dro increasing to 0.25 or 0.5. Extend estimate_loss to report ordinary train and validation loss as the primary metric, worst-group validation loss, all per-group validation losses, rare-novel loss, rare-repeated loss, learned group weights, train-val gap, total train time, and unchanged inference tokens per second. Compare against the baseline, fixed rarity-balanced weighting, and a random group-weight control across shakespeare_char, enwik8, and text8.
