# Title: State-Patch Sufficiency for Boundary-Robust Character Transformers
# Experiment description: Refactor GPT.forward to optionally stop after a chosen transformer block, return the residual stream, and continue from a supplied residual stream through the remaining blocks. Add a paired-view helper that preserves a recent suffix, such as the final 96 or 128 tokens, while replacing the older prefix with rolled same-batch prefixes. Train every iteration with ordinary next-character cross-entropy. After warmup_iters, every patch_interval iterations, run clean and prefix-replaced views up to one sampled patch layer, replace the prefix-replaced residual states on the shared suffix after a short burn-in with detached clean residual states, continue through the remaining layers, and add a small ramped temperature-2 KL loss from detached clean final logits to patched logits on the shared suffix. Keep default evaluation and generation unchanged. Extend diagnostics to sweep patch layers at validation time and report ordinary validation loss, prefix-replaced validation loss, patch recovery ratio by layer, patch recovery by distance into the shared suffix, clean-to-patched KL, train-val gap, total train time, and ordinary generation tokens per second. Compare against the baseline, prefix-replacement logit consistency, hidden-state suffix matching, and a shuffled-patch control across shakespeare_char, enwik8, and text8.
