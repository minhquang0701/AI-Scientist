# Title: Depthwise Innovation Budgets for Predictive Transformer Refinement
# Experiment description: Modify GPT.forward() to optionally return hidden states after selected transformer blocks, such as layers 2, 4, and the final layer. Add one LayerNorm probe per intermediate selected depth and reuse the tied lm_head to compute logit-lens predictions only on scheduled auxiliary iterations. Train with ordinary final-layer next-character cross-entropy on every iteration. After warmup_iters and every depth_innovation_interval iterations, compute intermediate probe losses with a small anchor weight, then compute softened predictive distributions at adjacent selected depths and measure KL(p_early.detach() || p_late) as depthwise predictive innovation. Define a detached per-token innovation budget from the earlier-depth entropy and target NLL, so confident easy positions receive a small budget while uncertain or wrong positions can change more through depth. Add a small ramped excess-innovation penalty mean(relu(KL - budget)^2), keeping final CE dominant. Evaluation and generation use only ordinary final-layer logits. Extend estimate_loss() to report final validation loss, intermediate probe losses, total depthwise predictive path length, budget violation rate, innovation by entropy bucket, expected calibration error, train-val gap, total train time, and unchanged final-layer inference tokens per second. Compare against the baseline, auxiliary intermediate CE only, uniform depthwise KL smoothing, and a shuffled-budget control across shakespeare_char, enwik8, and text8.
