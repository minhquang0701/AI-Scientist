# Title: Hidden-Manifold Confidence Caps for Calibrated Character Language Models
# Experiment description: Modify GPT.forward() to optionally return final hidden states after ln_f while keeping the default evaluation and generation path unchanged. In train(), maintain a FIFO train-only memory bank of detached L2-normalized final hidden states sampled from recent ordinary training batches, capped at a modest size such as 8192 or 16384 positions. Train every iteration with ordinary next-character cross-entropy. After warmup_iters and every density_interval iterations, subsample at most 1024 valid batch-time positions, compute their mean top-k cosine similarity to the memory bank in chunks, and treat this detached score as a hidden-manifold density estimate. Maintain an EMA low-density percentile, such as the 20th percentile, and add a weak one-sided confidence penalty only for positions below that threshold: low_density_gate * relu(max_prob - confidence_cap)^2, with confidence_cap around 0.90 and lambda_density ramped to 0.01 to 0.05. Update the memory bank only after the optimizer step using detached current hidden states. Extend estimate_loss() to report ordinary train/validation NLL as the primary metric, expected calibration error, Brier score, validation confidence by hidden-density bucket, low-density overconfidence rate, train-val gap, total train time, and unchanged generation tokens per second. Compare the baseline, uniform label smoothing, a global confidence-cap control, a shuffled-density control, and hidden-manifold confidence caps across shakespeare_char, enwik8, and text8.
