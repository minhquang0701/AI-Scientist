# Title: Local Marginal Calibration for Autoregressive Character Language Models
# Experiment description: Keep the GPT architecture, data loader, optimizer, evaluation path, and generation code unchanged. In the training loop, compute ordinary next-character cross-entropy as the primary loss. After warmup_iters, add a small ramped auxiliary loss that partitions each sequence into non-overlapping chunks at one or two scales such as 32 and 128 positions. For each chunk, average softmax(logits) over valid positions, compute a Dirichlet-smoothed empirical histogram of the target characters in that chunk, and minimize bounded Jensen-Shannon divergence between the predicted marginal and empirical marginal. Use conservative lambda_marginal values such as 0.02, 0.05, and 0.1 so token-level likelihood remains dominant. Extend estimate_loss() to report ordinary train and validation loss as the primary metric, local marginal divergence by scale, expected calibration error, validation loss by local target-entropy bucket, generated character-frequency KL against validation, repeated-character rate, and distinct n-gram fraction. Compare against the baseline, uniform label smoothing, global batch-level marginal matching, and shuffled-chunk histogram controls across shakespeare_char, enwik8, and text8. Since generation uses the unchanged LM head, inference tokens per second should remain essentially unchanged.
