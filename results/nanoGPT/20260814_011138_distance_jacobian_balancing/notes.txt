# Title: Distance-Balanced Jacobian Regularization for Context-Robust Character Language Models
# Experiment description: Modify GPT.forward to optionally accept embedding_delta added after token embedding lookup and before positional embeddings, preserving the default idx-only path for ordinary training, evaluation, and generation. Train every iteration with standard next-character cross-entropy. After warmup_iters and every jacobian_interval iterations, compute clean logits on the current batch, protect a recent suffix such as the final 96 or 128 tokens, and divide the older prefix into a few logarithmic distance bands such as near, middle, and far. For each auxiliary step, sample two or three bands and create small Gaussian embedding perturbations restricted to one band at a time, matched for total RMS energy per sequence. Run the perturbed forwards, compute temperature-2 KL from detached clean suffix logits to each perturbed suffix prediction, and treat these KL values as finite-difference sensitivity estimates by distance band. Add a small ramped concentration penalty, such as relu(max_band_sensitivity - sensitivity_mean - margin)^2 or an entropy-floor loss over normalized band sensitivities, so the model is discouraged from relying too sharply on one prefix distance band without forcing all sensitivities to zero. Extend estimate_loss to report ordinary train/validation loss, bandwise perturbation KL, sensitivity concentration, random prefix-erasure validation loss, train-val gap, total train time, and unchanged clean generation tokens per second. Compare against the baseline, uniform prefix-noise consistency, context-band masking consistency, and a shuffled-distance-band control across shakespeare_char, enwik8, and text8.
