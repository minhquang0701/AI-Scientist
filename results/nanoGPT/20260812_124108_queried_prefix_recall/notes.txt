# Title: Queried Prefix Recall for Memory-Rich Autoregressive Representations
# Experiment description: Modify GPT.forward() to optionally return final hidden states after ln_f. Add a small training-only prefix recall head that receives hidden state h_t plus a learned embedding of a sampled lookback-distance bucket and predicts the character X[:, t - d]. In the training loop, keep ordinary next-character cross-entropy as the primary objective on every iteration. After warmup_iters, every recall_interval iterations, sample at most 512 valid batch-time positions and one log-balanced lookback distance per position from buckets such as 1-4, 5-16, 17-64, and 65-255. Add a ramped auxiliary recall cross-entropy with lambda_recall capped at 0.05 or 0.1. Evaluation and generation ignore the recall head and use the normal lm_head path. Extend estimate_loss() to report ordinary train/validation loss, recall accuracy by distance bucket, validation loss by previous-occurrence distance of the next target, repeated-target versus novel-target loss, train-val gap, total train time, and unchanged inference tokens per second. Compare against the baseline, a shuffled-recall-label control, and a short-distance-only recall control to test whether explicit queryable prefix memory improves language modeling beyond auxiliary-computation effects.
