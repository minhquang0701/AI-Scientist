# Title: Dominant-Subspace Consistency for Representation-Robust Character Transformers
# Experiment description: Modify GPT.forward() to optionally return final hidden states after ln_f, and add a logits_from_hidden helper using the tied lm_head. During training, maintain stop-gradient EMA estimates of the final-hidden mean and covariance from subsampled batch-time positions. Every refresh interval, eigendecompose the n_embd by n_embd covariance matrix and store the top principal directions as buffers. Train every iteration with ordinary next-character cross-entropy. After warmup_iters and every subspace_interval iterations, compute clean logits and hidden states once, attenuate a small random subset of the current top principal directions in the hidden states, and compute perturbed logits from the modified hidden states without rerunning the transformer. Add a small ramped auxiliary loss combining perturbed CE and temperature-2 KL consistency from detached clean logits to perturbed logits. Extend estimate_loss to report ordinary validation loss, top-PC attenuation validation loss, random-subspace attenuation loss, bottom-PC attenuation loss, effective hidden rank, top-PC energy fraction, expected calibration error, train-val gap, total train time, and unchanged clean generation tokens per second. Compare against the baseline, isotropic hidden jitter consistency, random-subspace consistency, and hidden-correlation regularization across shakespeare_char, enwik8, and text8.
