# Title: Transition-Marginal Consistency for Autoregressive Character Models
# Experiment description: Before training each dataset, scan train.bin once to build a smoothed empirical transition matrix P(x_{t+2} | x_{t+1}) over character ids, interpolating low-support rows with the unigram distribution. Keep the GPT architecture, batching, optimizer, ordinary next-character cross-entropy, evaluation path, and generation code unchanged. Add a helper transition_marginal_nll(logits, two_step_targets, log_transition) that uses log_softmax(logits[:, :-1, :]) and computes -logsumexp_c log p_model(c | prefix_t) + log P(x_{t+2} | c), where two_step_targets are Y[:, 1:]. In the training loop, after warmup_iters and every transition_interval iterations, optimize total_loss = ce_loss + lambda_transition * transition_marginal_nll, with lambda_transition ramped conservatively to values such as 0.02, 0.05, and 0.1. Extend estimate_loss to report ordinary train and validation loss as the primary metric, transition-marginal validation NLL, rollout calibration error, expected calibration error, train-val gap, total train time, and unchanged generation tokens per second. Compare against the baseline, unigram-transition control, shuffled-transition control, and an identity-transition control across shakespeare_char, enwik8, and text8.
