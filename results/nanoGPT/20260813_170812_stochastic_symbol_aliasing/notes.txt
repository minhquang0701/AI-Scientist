Add alias_marginal_ce(logits, targets, alias_factor), which reshapes logits to batch by time by original_vocab_size by K, applies logsumexp over aliases, and computes cross-entropy against canonical targets. For K=1 this recovers the baseline exactly. Modify estimate_loss() to average validation loss across several independent alias redraws and log alias-sensitivity KL or JS divergence between predictions for different alias encodings of the same batch. Modify generation to marginalize alias logits into canonical character logits, sample a canonical character, and feed back either alias 0 or a random alias of that character. Compare the K=1 baseline, stochastic aliasing with K in {2, 4}, an expanded-vocabulary alias-0-only capacity control, and an alias-specific CE control that predicts sampled aliases instead of marginalizing them. Report best character-level validation loss, train-val gap, calibration, alias sensitivity, generation diagnostics, total train time, and character-level inference tokens per second across shakespeare_char, enwik8, and text8.
