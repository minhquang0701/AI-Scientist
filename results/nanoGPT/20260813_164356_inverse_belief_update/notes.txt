# Title: Inverse Belief-Update Regularization for Reversible Autoregressive State Dynamics
# Experiment description: Modify GPT.forward() to optionally return final hidden states after ln_f in addition to logits and loss. Add a small training-only inverse_update module that takes concat(hidden[:, 1:, :], detached token_embedding(X[:, 1:])) and predicts a reconstructed previous predictive state. Apply the tied lm_head to this reconstructed state and train it to match the detached previous-step predictive distribution logits[:, :-1, :] using temperature-2 KL divergence. Keep ordinary next-character cross-entropy as the primary loss on every iteration. After warmup_iters, every inverse_interval iterations such as 4 or 8, add lambda_inverse times the inverse predictive KL, ramping lambda_inverse conservatively to 0.02, 0.05, or 0.1. Evaluation and generation ignore the inverse module and use the standard lm_head path unchanged. Extend estimate_loss() to report ordinary train/validation loss, inverse predictive KL, adjacent predictive JS drift, expected calibration error, validation loss at context lengths 64 and 256, train-val gap, total train time, and unchanged inference tokens per second. Compare against the baseline, a forward belief-update auxiliary, a shuffled-observed-token inverse control, and a parameter-matched auxiliary MLP control across shakespeare_char, enwik8, and text8.
