# Title: Gated Token-Conditioned Belief Transition Consistency for Autoregressive Language Models
# Experiment description: Keep the GPT architecture, data loader, ordinary next-character cross-entropy objective, evaluation, and generation path unchanged. Add a small training-only TokenBeliefTransition module, either inside GPT or alongside it with its parameters included in the optimizer. Given detached log probabilities at position t and the observed token X[:, t + 1], the module predicts logits for the model's next predictive distribution at position t + 1 using a lightweight token-conditioned MLP or low-rank residual map. In train(), always optimize the standard CE loss. After warmup_iters and every transition_interval iterations, compute log_probs = log_softmax(logits / T) from the normal forward pass. Train the transition module to match detached log_probs[:, 1:, :] from detached log_probs[:, :-1, :] and X[:, 1:] using temperature-scaled KL. Then add a weak ramped consistency loss from the detached transition prediction to the model's actual log_probs[:, 1:, :], gated only on positions where the transition predictor improves over a detached no-change predictor log_probs[:, :-1, :]. Keep lambda_transition conservative, such as 0.01 to 0.05. Extend estimate_loss to report ordinary train and validation loss as the primary metric, transition prediction KL, no-change KL, shuffled-observed-token KL, adjacent predictive JS drift, expected calibration error, train-val gap, total train time, and unchanged inference tokens per second. Compare against the baseline, probe-only transition prediction without GPT consistency, token-agnostic transitions, and shuffled-observed-token controls across shakespeare_char, enwik8, and text8.
