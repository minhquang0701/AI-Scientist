# Title: Forecast-Tape CTC for Alignment-Free Future Prediction in Causal Language Models
# Experiment description: Modify GPT.forward() to optionally return final hidden states after ln_f. Add a training-only future_ctc_head Linear(n_embd, vocab_size + 1), where the extra class is a CTC blank. Train every iteration with the ordinary next-character cross-entropy. After warmup_iters and every ctc_interval iterations, run the normal forward pass with return_hidden=True, sample a prefix boundary such as 128, 160, or 192 and a future length such as 16 or 32, ensuring prefix_len + future_len <= block_size. Use only the forecast tape hidden[:, prefix_len - tape_len:prefix_len, :] with tape_len around 2 to 3 times future_len, feed it through future_ctc_head, and apply torch.nn.CTCLoss(blank=vocab_size, zero_infinity=True) to the future target string Y[:, prefix_len - 1:prefix_len - 1 + future_len]. Add the CTC loss with a small ramped weight such as lambda_ctc up to 0.05 or 0.15 while keeping ordinary CE dominant. Evaluation and generation ignore the CTC head and use the standard lm_head. Extend estimate_loss() to report ordinary validation loss as the primary metric, forecast-CTC validation loss, greedy CTC future-string edit distance, train-val gap, total train time, and unchanged generation tokens per second. Compare against the baseline, fixed-horizon auxiliary future heads, shuffled-future CTC targets, and tape-length ablations across shakespeare_char, enwik8, and text8.
