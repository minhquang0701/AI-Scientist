# Title: Hindsight Future-Neighbor Supervision for Predictive Character States
# Experiment description: Modify GPT.forward to optionally return final hidden states after ln_f. Keep the data loader, generation path, and ordinary next-character cross-entropy objective unchanged. After warmup_iters, every neighbor_interval iterations, run the normal forward pass with return_hidden=True and build training-only future sketches from the existing targets: for each valid position t, summarize the next H target characters Y[:, t:t+H] using a fixed normalized random token-feature table and discounted averaging, masking positions too close to the right edge. Subsample at most 512 valid batch-time positions, L2-normalize future sketches and hidden states, compute future-sketch cosine similarities, mask self-pairs and overlapping nearby positions from the same sequence, and convert each row's top-k future neighbors into a detached soft target distribution. Compute hidden-neighbor log probabilities from hidden-state cosine similarities and add a small ramped KL loss from future-neighbor targets to hidden-neighbor predictions. Evaluation and generation ignore the auxiliary machinery. Extend estimate_loss to report ordinary train/validation loss as the primary metric, future-neighbor retrieval accuracy from hidden states, hidden/future similarity rank correlation, train-val gap, total train time, and inference tokens per second. Compare against the baseline, shuffled future-sketch targets, one-positive future-chunk contrastive supervision, and a random-neighbor auxiliary control across shakespeare_char, enwik8, and text8.
