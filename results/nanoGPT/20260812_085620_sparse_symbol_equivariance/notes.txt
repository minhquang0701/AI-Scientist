# Title: Sparse Symbol Equivariance for Tokenizer-Free Language Models
# Experiment description: Before training each dataset, scan train.bin to compute token frequencies and derive protected structural token ids from meta.pkl, such as whitespace, newline, tabs, and common punctuation when present. Partition remaining tokens into coarse frequency buckets. Add a helper that samples a sparse bijection by swapping only a small fraction of non-protected tokens within each bucket, leaving all other tokens unchanged. In the training loop, always optimize the ordinary clean next-character cross-entropy on X, Y. After warmup_iters, every swap_interval iterations, sample a sparse swap map, construct X_swap and Y_swap by applying it to X and Y, run a second forward pass, and add a ramped auxiliary loss: swapped-view cross-entropy plus a temperature-2 KL consistency loss between clean logits permuted into swapped-token space and swapped logits. Keep standard evaluation and generation unchanged. Extend estimate_loss to report clean train/validation loss, validation loss under fixed unseen sparse swaps, swap-consistency KL, rare-token validation loss, train-val gap, total train time, and unchanged inference tokens per second. Compare the baseline, sparse frequency-bucket swaps, stronger full-bucket permutations, swapped-CE-only, and an input-only swap control where targets remain canonical.
