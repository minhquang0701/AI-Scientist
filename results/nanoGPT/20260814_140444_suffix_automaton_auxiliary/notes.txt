# Title: Causal Suffix-Automaton Signals for Compression-Aware Character Language Modeling
# Experiment description: Modify GPT.forward() to optionally return final hidden states after ln_f, and add small training-only auxiliary heads for bucketed suffix-automaton signals. Add compute_suffix_automaton_targets(X), used only on scheduled auxiliary iterations, that scans each sequence left to right on CPU and builds an online suffix automaton over the visible prefix. For each input position, record three causal labels: longest repeated suffix length bucket from len(link(last)), continuation-branching bucket from the outgoing transitions of that suffix-link state, and suffix-link depth bucket as a multiscale reuse signal. Train every iteration with ordinary next-character cross-entropy; after warmup_iters and every automaton_interval iterations, run the normal forward pass with return_hidden=True and add a small ramped auxiliary cross-entropy over these labels, with lambda_automaton in the range 0.02 to 0.10. Evaluation and generation ignore the auxiliary heads and use the standard lm_head unchanged. Extend estimate_loss() to report ordinary train and validation loss as the primary metric, automaton-label accuracy, validation loss by repeated-suffix and branching buckets, repeated-substring versus non-repeated loss, train-val gap, total train time, and unchanged inference tokens per second. Compare against the baseline, shuffled-label controls, prefix-function auxiliary controls, and LZ-match auxiliary controls across shakespeare_char, enwik8, and text8.
