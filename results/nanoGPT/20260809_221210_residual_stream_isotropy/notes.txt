# Title: Residual Stream Isotropy Regularization for Autoregressive Transformers
# Experiment description: Add a lightweight training-only isotropy regularizer on GPT hidden states. Extend GPTConfig with use_isotropy_regularizer, isotropy_loss_weight, isotropy_sample_positions, isotropy_ramp_iters, isotropy_variance_floor, and isotropy_corr_weight. Modify GPT.forward to optionally return the final residual stream after ln_f and before lm_head while preserving the existing logits and loss behavior. Implement residual_isotropy_loss(hidden) by randomly sampling batch-time positions, casting to float32, centering features, penalizing per-dimension standard deviation below isotropy_variance_floor, then standardizing dimensions and penalizing squared off-diagonal entries of the resulting correlation matrix. In the training loop, keep ordinary next-character cross-entropy as the main objective and add a ramped auxiliary loss after warmup_iters: total_loss = ce_loss + alpha_t * isotropy_loss. Keep estimate_loss, validation, generation, final reporting, and inference speed based on the ordinary next-character lm_head for comparability. Compare the baseline against final-layer isotropy weights such as 1e-4, 3e-4, and 1e-3 across shakespeare_char, enwik8, and text8. Include a small ablation that also applies the same penalty to one middle layer. Report best validation loss, convergence speed, total train time, inference tokens per second, generated samples, effective rank of hidden states, mean absolute off-diagonal correlation, and fraction of low-variance dimensions.
