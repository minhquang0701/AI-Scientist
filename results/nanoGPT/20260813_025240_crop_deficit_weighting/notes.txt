# Title: Crop-Deficit Weighted Training for Missing-Context-Aware Autoregressive Language Models
# Experiment description: Modify GPT.forward() to optionally return final hidden states after ln_f, and add a small optional crop_deficit_head Linear(n_embd, 1) used only during training and diagnostics. Modify get_batch() on auxiliary iterations to sample chunks of length block_size + extra_left + 1. From each chunk, create an extended-context view X_ctx = chunk[:, :block_size], Y_ctx = chunk[:, 1:block_size + 1] and a cropped view X_crop = chunk[:, extra_left:extra_left + block_size], Y_crop = chunk[:, extra_left + 1:extra_left + block_size + 1]. The aligned predictions are crop positions 0 through block_size - extra_left - 1 and context positions extra_left through block_size - 1, which correspond to the same absolute target characters but different left-context lengths. Train ordinary batches with the standard next-character cross-entropy. After warmup_iters, every deficit_interval iterations, run both aligned views, compute detached deficit = clamp(nll_crop - nll_ctx, min=0, max=deficit_cap) / deficit_cap, and optimize an auxiliary cropped-view loss whose per-token weights are reduced for high-deficit positions with a floor such as 0.25 and normalized to mean 1. Also train crop_deficit_head(hidden_crop) to predict the detached deficit with MSE or BCE, with a small ramped weight. Keep evaluation and generation through the ordinary lm_head unchanged. Extend estimate_loss() to report ordinary validation loss, interior-suffix validation loss, validation loss by absolute-position bucket, aligned crop-to-context NLL gap, crop-deficit prediction MSE or AUROC, boundary-to-interior loss gap, and effective supervised-token weight. Compare against the baseline, fixed context-ramp loss weighting, overlap-window KL consistency, and a shuffled-deficit weighting control across shakespeare_char, enwik8, and text8.
