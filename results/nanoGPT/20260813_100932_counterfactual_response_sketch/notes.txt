# Title: Counterfactual Response Sketching for Causal Sensitivity in Character Language Models
# Experiment description: Modify GPT.forward() to optionally return final hidden states after ln_f while keeping the default training, evaluation, and generation paths unchanged. Add a small training-only counterfactual_response_head plus a logarithmic relative-distance embedding, and initialize a fixed random projection matrix R of shape vocab_size by sketch_dim. In train(), always optimize ordinary next-character cross-entropy. After warmup_iters, every counterfactual_interval iterations, choose one older-prefix position per sequence before a protected suffix such as the final 96 or 128 tokens, replace that token with a plausible alternative sampled from the clean model top-k prediction at the preceding position or from a smoothed unigram fallback, and run one matched counterfactual probe forward. For protected suffix positions, compute detached delta_sketch = (counterfactual_logits - clean_logits) @ R. Feed the clean suffix hidden states, a detached replacement-minus-original token embedding, and the relative-distance embedding into counterfactual_response_head, and train it with Huber or MSE loss to predict delta_sketch. Ramp a small auxiliary weight after warmup so ordinary likelihood remains dominant. Evaluation and generation use only the ordinary lm_head. Extend estimate_loss() to report ordinary train/validation loss, counterfactual response sketch error, clean-to-counterfactual suffix KL, validation loss by previous-occurrence distance, repeated-target versus novel-target loss, train-val gap, total train time, and unchanged generation tokens per second. Compare against the baseline, shuffled intervention metadata, random delta targets, and a detached-hidden auxiliary-head diagnostic across shakespeare_char, enwik8, and text8.
