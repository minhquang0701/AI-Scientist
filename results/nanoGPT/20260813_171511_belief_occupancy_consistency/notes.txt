# Title: Belief-Occupancy Consistency for Short-Horizon Autoregressive Calibration
# Experiment description: Before training each dataset, scan train.bin once to build a smoothed bigram transition matrix over character ids. Modify GPT.forward() to optionally return final hidden states after ln_f, and add a training-only occupancy_head Linear(n_embd, vocab_size). On auxiliary iterations, sample block_size + H + 1 tokens with a fixed default such as H=16 and discount gamma=0.9. Train with ordinary next-character cross-entropy on every iteration. After warmup_iters and every occupancy_interval iterations, compute ordinary logits and hidden states, form p1 = softmax(logits).detach(), propagate p1 through the fixed bigram transition matrix for H steps, and average the resulting distributions with discounted weights to obtain a detached belief-rollout occupancy target. Also build an empirical discounted future-character occupancy target from the actual next H tokens. Train occupancy_head(hidden_states) with a small ramped soft cross-entropy or KL loss to match a convex mixture of the empirical target and belief-rollout target, while keeping the ordinary LM loss dominant. Evaluation and generation ignore the occupancy head and use the standard lm_head unchanged. Extend estimate_loss() to report ordinary validation loss as the primary metric, occupancy KL, expected calibration error, repeated n-gram rate, distinct n-gram fraction, and validation loss by predictive-entropy bucket. Compare the baseline, empirical-occupancy-only auxiliary, rollout-occupancy-only auxiliary, mixed belief-occupancy consistency, and shuffled-future controls across shakespeare_char, enwik8, and text8.
