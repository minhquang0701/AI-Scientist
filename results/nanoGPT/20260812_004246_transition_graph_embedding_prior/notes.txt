# Title: Transition-Graph Embedding Priors for Character Language Models
# Experiment description: Before training each dataset, scan train.bin once to compute smoothed predecessor and successor distributions for every character. Concatenate these distributions into one context vector per character, compute pairwise token similarities, exclude self-neighbors, and keep a top-k row-normalized transition-neighbor graph with a small uniform smoothing floor for stability. Add an embedding_graph_loss helper that L2-normalizes the tied token embedding matrix model.transformer.wte.weight, forms token-token similarity logits divided by a temperature, masks the diagonal, and minimizes KL divergence from the fixed transition graph to the embedding-induced neighbor distribution. Keep the ordinary next-character cross-entropy objective on every iteration; after warmup_iters, add lambda_graph * embedding_graph_loss every graph_interval iterations, ramping lambda_graph to a small value such as 0.001 to 0.01. Evaluation and generation remain unchanged. Log ordinary train/validation loss as the primary metric, plus embedding graph KL, rare-token validation loss, train-val gap, total train time, and inference tokens per second. Compare against the baseline, a random-neighbor graph control, and a unigram-frequency-only graph control across shakespeare_char, enwik8, and text8, with small ablations over top-k and lambda_graph.
