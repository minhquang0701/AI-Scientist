# Title: Belief-Velocity Alignment for Predictive-State Geometry in Character Transformers
# Experiment description: Modify GPT.forward() to optionally return final hidden states after ln_f. Add a belief_velocity_alignment_loss(hidden, logits, targets, burnin=8) helper. On scheduled auxiliary iterations after warmup_iters, compute detached consecutive belief velocities using squared Hellinger distance between softmax(logits[:, t - 1]) and softmax(logits[:, t]). Compute trainable hidden velocities as squared distances between consecutive L2-normalized hidden states. Use only positions t >= burnin where both adjacent targets are valid. Z-score both velocity sequences within the batch with an epsilon for stability, then penalize their mismatch with a Huber loss or equivalently maximize their correlation. Train every iteration with ordinary next-character cross-entropy, and every velocity_interval iterations add lambda_velocity times this auxiliary loss, ramping lambda_velocity to small values such as 0.005, 0.01, and 0.02. Include controls with shuffled belief velocities, hidden-smoothing regularization, and no auxiliary loss. Extend estimate_loss() to report ordinary train and validation loss as the primary metric, hidden-belief velocity correlation, velocity alignment loss, expected calibration error, validation loss by belief-velocity bucket, train-val gap, total train time, and unchanged generation tokens per second across shakespeare_char, enwik8, and text8.
