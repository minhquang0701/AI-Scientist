# Title: Depth-Velocity Extrapolation for Cheap Predictive Refinement in Character Transformers
# Experiment description: Modify GPT.forward to optionally retain the penultimate and final residual streams before the final LayerNorm. Add a logits_from_hidden helper and one learned bounded scalar gamma initialized to zero, parameterized for example as gamma_max * tanh(raw_gamma). When enabled, compute h_extra = h_final + gamma * (h_final - h_penultimate), then apply the existing final LayerNorm and tied lm_head to obtain extrapolated logits. Keep the disabled path exactly identical to the baseline. Train with ordinary base-logit cross-entropy as an anchor on every iteration; after warmup_iters, add a ramped extrapolated-logit cross-entropy term plus a small gamma^2 penalty. Evaluation should report base validation loss, extrapolated validation loss, base-to-extrapolated KL, learned gamma, expected calibration error, validation gains by position bucket and predictive-entropy bucket, total train time, and base versus extrapolated generation tokens per second. Compare against the original baseline, a fixed small gamma ablation, a learned global temperature control, and depth log-probability fusion across shakespeare_char, enwik8, and text8.
