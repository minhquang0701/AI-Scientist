# Title: Adversarial Position Scrubbing for Translation-Robust Autoregressive Transformers
# Experiment description: Add a lightweight training-only adversarial classifier that discourages final hidden states from making absolute block position linearly decodable. Implement a GradReverse autograd function and extend GPTConfig with use_position_adversary, position_adv_weight, position_adv_ramp_iters, and position_adv_sample_positions. In GPT.__init__, add pos_adv_head = nn.Linear(n_embd, block_size). Modify GPT.forward to optionally return final hidden states after ln_f. In the training loop, keep the ordinary next-character cross-entropy as the primary loss. After warmup_iters, randomly sample up to position_adv_sample_positions batch-time hidden states, pass them through gradient reversal with a ramped coefficient, predict their absolute timestep with pos_adv_head, and add the resulting position classification loss to the training objective. The classifier learns to predict position, while the Transformer trunk receives reversed gradients scaled by the ramped adversarial weight. Keep estimate_loss, clean validation, final reporting, inference speed, and generation based only on the standard lm_head. Add diagnostics for adversarial position accuracy, a detached validation position-probe accuracy, cropped-context validation losses at 64 and 128 tokens, and shifted-position validation losses using an optional position_offset argument in GPT.forward. Compare the baseline against position_adv_weight values such as 0.01, 0.03, and 0.1 across shakespeare_char, enwik8, and text8, reporting best clean validation loss, robustness to shifted and cropped contexts, convergence speed, total train time, inference tokens per second, generated samples, and whether reduced position decodability correlates with better context robustness.
