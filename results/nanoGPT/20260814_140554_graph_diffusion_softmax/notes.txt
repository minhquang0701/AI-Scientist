Add a logits_from_hidden helper in GPT: compute ordinary tied-softmax logits and probabilities, then when enabled compute one-step lazy graph diffusion p_diff = p_base P. Predict a bounded per-token mixture gate from final hidden states, initialized to zero, and form p_mix = (1 - gate) * p_base + gate * p_diff. Train with NLL on log(p_mix), plus a small ordinary base-softmax CE anchor and optional gate L2 after warmup to discourage unnecessary smoothing. Modify estimate_loss and generate to use logits_from_hidden, while also reporting base-only validation loss with graph diffusion disabled. Compare the baseline, fixed global diffusion gate, context-adaptive gate, random graph control, and uniform label smoothing across shakespeare_char, enwik8, and text8.
