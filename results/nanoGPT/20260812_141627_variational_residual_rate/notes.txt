# Title: Variational Residual Rate Bottlenecks for Predictive Character Transformers
# Experiment description: Add an optional VariationalResidualBottleneck after one configurable middle transformer block, for example block 3 in the 6-layer model. Extend GPTConfig with use_variational_residual_bottleneck, bottleneck_layer, bottleneck_dim, bottleneck_mix_max, kl_beta_max, and kl_free_nats. The bottleneck applies LayerNorm to hidden states, predicts mu and logvar in bottleneck_dim, samples z = mu + eps * std during training, projects z back to n_embd with a zero-initialized output projection, and adds it residually as h = h + mix * z_proj. The mix value ramps from 0.0 after warmup_iters to bottleneck_mix_max, so the model starts exactly as the baseline. Add a per-token KL(q(z|h) || N(0,I)) term with free-nats clipping, and optimize ordinary next-character cross-entropy plus ramped beta_kl times the KL. Evaluation and generation use the deterministic mean path by default. Extend estimate_loss to report ordinary validation loss, mean KL rate per token, validation loss by KL-rate bucket, train-val gap, deterministic versus stochastic validation loss, total train time, and inference tokens per second. Compare the baseline, residual bottleneck without KL, variational residual bottlenecks with bottleneck_dim values such as n_embd / 2 and n_embd / 4, and a matched Gaussian residual-noise control across shakespeare_char, enwik8, and text8.
