# Title: Context-Gain Weighted Language Modeling: Focusing Training on Tokens That Need Long Context
# Experiment description: Keep the GPT architecture and generation code unchanged. In the training loop, compute the normal full-context logits for X, Y and the ordinary mean next-token cross-entropy loss. After warmup_iters, every k iterations such as k=4, also evaluate a short-context suffix X[:, -L:], Y[:, -L:] with L=64 under torch.no_grad(). Compute unreduced per-token cross-entropy for the aligned final L positions from both the full-context logits and the short-context logits. Estimate detached context_gain = short_loss - full_loss, convert positive gains into weights with weights = 1 + gain_scale * clamp(context_gain, min=0), cap weights at 3.0, and normalize them to mean 1. Add a ramped auxiliary weighted loss on the final L full-context positions, for example total_loss = ordinary_loss + aux_weight * weighted_suffix_loss with aux_weight ramping from 0 to 0.3 after warmup. On non-auxiliary iterations, train exactly like the baseline. Extend estimate_loss to report ordinary validation loss at context lengths 32, 64, 128, and 256, plus mean positive context gain and validation loss on high-gain versus low-gain token positions. Compare against the baseline using best full-context validation loss, mean multi-context validation loss, convergence speed, total train time, and inference tokens per second.
