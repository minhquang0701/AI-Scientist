# Title: Boundary-Position Gradient Surgery for Crop-Robust Character Transformers
# Experiment description: Modify the training loop to compute unreduced next-character cross-entropy from logits. On scheduled surgery iterations after warmup_iters, split token losses into a low-context boundary region such as the first 32 or 64 positions and an interior region. Use torch.autograd.grad to compute separate boundary-loss and interior-loss gradients only for transformer.wpe.weight. After the normal backward pass and GradScaler unscale, replace the positional embedding gradient with interior_grad plus a conservatively edited boundary_grad: for rows or flattened components where boundary and interior gradients have negative cosine, project the boundary component away from the conflicting interior direction or scale it toward a floor. Leave all non-position parameters unchanged. On non-surgery iterations, train exactly like the baseline. Disable torch.compile for this experiment if separate positional gradients are unreliable. Compare the baseline, boundary-position gradient surgery, random-direction surgery, fixed context-ramp loss weighting, and simple position-gradient clipping across shakespeare_char, enwik8, and text8. Extend estimate_loss to report ordinary validation loss, validation loss by absolute-position bucket, boundary-to-interior loss gap, validation losses at context lengths 32/64/128/256, boundary-interior positional-gradient cosine, projection rate, total train time, and unchanged generation tokens per second.
