# Experiment description: Extend GPTConfig with use_successor_distribution, successor_gammas, successor_loss_weight, and successor_ramp_iters. In the training loop, keep next-token cross-entropy as the primary loss. Use the immediate one-hot target or ignore_index handling for tail positions. Keep estimate_loss, best validation loss, generation, final reporting, and inference speed based only on the ordinary lm_head. Add diagnostics for successor TD loss, successor entropy, agreement with empirical finite-window future histograms, and validation loss stratified by whether the next token recurs soon.
