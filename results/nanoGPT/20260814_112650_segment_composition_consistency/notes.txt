# Title: Segment Composition Consistency for Chunk-Reusable Character Language Models
# Experiment description: Modify GPT.forward to optionally return final hidden states after ln_f and optionally accept position_ids so isolated chunks can be encoded at their original absolute positions. Add a small train-only SegmentComposer MLP, included in the optimizer, that takes two segment endpoint hidden states plus learned segment-length embeddings and predicts the hidden state that the full model would have produced after reading the two segments contiguously. Train every iteration with the ordinary next-character cross-entropy. After warmup_iters and every composition_interval iterations, sample a split point such as 64, 128, or 192, run the normal full-window forward with return_hidden=True, then run isolated forwards on the left and right chunks with matched position_ids. Use the isolated endpoint states as composer inputs and match the composed state to the detached full-window endpoint state with a small normalized MSE or cosine loss. Also apply the tied lm_head to the composed state and add a small temperature-2 KL loss to the detached full-window boundary logits. Keep generation and ordinary evaluation unchanged. Extend estimate_loss to report ordinary validation loss, composed-boundary NLL, hidden composition error, train-val gap, total train time, and unchanged generation tokens per second. Compare against the baseline, a probe-only composer control where isolated hidden states are detached so GPT representations are not shaped, a shuffled-segment control, and a compute-matched extra-forward control across shakespeare_char, enwik8, and text8.
