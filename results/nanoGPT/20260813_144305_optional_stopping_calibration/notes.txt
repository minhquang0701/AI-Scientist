# Title: Optional-Stopping Calibration for Adaptively Queried Character Language Models
# Experiment description: Keep the GPT architecture, data loader, optimizer, evaluation path, and generation code unchanged. Add helpers that construct detached stopping-time masks from model predictions within each sequence, such as the first position after a burn-in where top-1 confidence exceeds a threshold, the first position where entropy falls below a threshold, and fixed-position controls. In the training loop, always optimize ordinary next-character cross-entropy. After warmup_iters and every calibration_interval iterations, add a small ramped calibration penalty only on stopped positions: for each stopping rule with enough selected examples, compute mean predicted top-1 confidence and detached empirical top-1 accuracy, then penalize their squared gap. Extend estimate_loss to report ordinary train and validation NLL as the primary metric, standard ECE, stopped ECE under training and held-out stopping rules, stopped accuracy-confidence gaps, selective risk-coverage curves, average stopping position, and train-val gap. Compare the baseline, ordinary confidence calibration on all positions, optional-stopping calibration, and shuffled stopping-mask controls across shakespeare_char, enwik8, and text8. Generation remains unchanged, so inference tokens per second should be unaffected.
