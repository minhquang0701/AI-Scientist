# Title: Predictability-Shift Auxiliary Learning for Autoregressive Language Models
# Experiment description: Modify GPT.forward() to optionally return final hidden states after ln_f. Add a small predictability_shift_head Linear(n_embd, 3) used only during training and diagnostics, with classes corresponding to future-easier, stable, and future-harder. In the training loop, keep ordinary next-character cross-entropy as the primary objective on every iteration, but compute unreduced per-token CE from logits. After warmup_iters, every shift_interval iterations such as 4 or 8, build detached labels from local loss dynamics: compare the mean CE over the next H positions to the mean CE over the previous H positions, with H such as 8 or 16, and assign future-harder if the difference exceeds a margin, future-easier if it is below the negative margin, and stable otherwise. Mask positions without enough past or future context. Add a small ramped auxiliary cross-entropy loss with lambda_shift up to 0.02 to 0.1. Include controls with shuffled shift labels and absolute future-risk labels. Evaluation and generation use only the ordinary lm_head. Extend estimate_loss() to report ordinary train/validation loss as the primary metric, predictability-shift accuracy, validation loss near predicted and true harder/easier regions, expected calibration error, train-val gap, total train time, and unchanged inference tokens per second across shakespeare_char, enwik8, and text8.
