# Title: Predictive-Change Budgets for Event-Aware Autoregressive Stability
# Experiment description: Modify GPT.forward() to optionally return final hidden states after ln_f. Add a small training-only predictive_change_head Linear(n_embd, num_change_horizons), with horizons such as 1, 2, and 4. Train every iteration with ordinary next-character cross-entropy. After warmup_iters and every change_interval iterations, compute detached predictive distributions from the current forward pass and build future-change targets: for each valid position t and horizon h, compute bounded Jensen-Shannon divergence between softmax(logits[:, t, :]) and softmax(logits[:, t + h, :]), masking positions without enough future context. Train the change head with Huber or MSE loss on these detached targets. Add a small ramped stability-budget loss on adjacent predictions, for example relu(JS(p_t, p_{t+1}) - detach(change_target_h1) - margin)^2, so predictable regions are smoothed while naturally high-change regions remain free to update sharply. Keep evaluation and generation unchanged and ignore the change head at inference. Extend estimate_loss() to report ordinary train/validation loss as the primary metric, predictive-change prediction error, adjacent predictive JS by change bucket, budget violation rate, validation loss near high-change versus low-change regions, expected calibration error, train-val gap, total train time, and unchanged generation tokens per second. Compare against the baseline, ungated adjacent-prediction smoothing, entropy-gated smoothing, shuffled-change-target controls, and a detached-head-only diagnostic across shakespeare_char, enwik8, and text8.
