# Title: Hashed Future N-Gram Sketch Prediction for Tokenization-Free Language Models
# Experiment description: Add a training-only auxiliary objective that asks each hidden state to predict a compact hashed sketch of short n-grams appearing in its near future. Extend GPTConfig with use_future_ngram_sketching, sketch_ngram_orders, sketch_window, sketch_num_buckets, sketch_loss_weight, and sketch_ramp_iters. In GPT.__init__, add one Linear(n_embd, sketch_num_buckets) sketch head per selected n-gram order, using orders [2, 3], sketch_window=16, and sketch_num_buckets=512 as the main setting. Modify GPT.forward to optionally return final hidden states before the lm_head while preserving the standard logits and next-character cross-entropy behavior. Add a future_ngram_sketch_loss helper that builds multi-hot bucket targets from targets: for each position, hash every valid future n-gram of the chosen order starting within the next sketch_window characters into a bucket, then compute a balanced BCEWithLogits loss that upweights positive buckets and ignores tail positions with no valid future n-grams. In the training loop, after warmup_iters, ramp alpha_t to sketch_loss_weight and optimize total_loss = next_token_loss + alpha_t * mean(sketch_losses). Keep estimate_loss, clean validation, final reporting, generation, and inference speed based on ordinary next-character loss for comparability. Add diagnostics for sketch validation BCE, top-k bucket recall, clean best validation loss, convergence speed, total training time, generated samples, and auxiliary parameter overhead. Compare the baseline against sketch_window values 16 and 32, sketch_num_buckets values 512 and 1024, and sketch_loss_weight values 0.02 and 0.05 across shakespeare_char, enwik8, and text8.
