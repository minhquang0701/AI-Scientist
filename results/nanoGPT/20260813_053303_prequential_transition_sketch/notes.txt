# Title: Prequential Transition-Sketch Calibration for Autoregressive Character Models
# Experiment description: Keep the GPT architecture, data loader, optimizer, evaluation path, and generation code unchanged. After loading each dataset's vocab_size, create two fixed zero-mean normalized random feature tables R and S of shape vocab_size by sketch_dim, with sketch_dim such as 16 or 32. Add a prequential_transition_sketch_loss(logits, targets, R, S, chunk_len=64) helper: compute probabilities from logits, take adjacent valid target pairs targets[:, :-1] and targets[:, 1:], map the first observed target to a context sketch R[y_t], map the model's next-position forecast to probs[:, 1:, :] @ S, and map the observed next target to S[y_{t+1}]. Within non-overlapping chunks, center the context sketches and next-token sketches by their chunk means, form predicted and observed transition moments as average outer products, and penalize their Huber or mean-squared difference. Train every iteration with ordinary next-character cross-entropy; after warmup_iters and every transition_sketch_interval iterations, add a small ramped lambda_transition_sketch times this loss, with lambda values such as 0.01, 0.02, and 0.05. Extend estimate_loss to report ordinary train and validation NLL as the primary metric, transition-sketch calibration error, local unigram marginal error, generated bigram JS against validation text, repeated n-gram rate, distinct n-gram fraction, train-val gap, total train time, and unchanged inference tokens per second. Compare against the baseline, local unigram marginal calibration, an uncentered transition-sketch ablation, and a shuffled-next-target control that preserves unigram frequencies across shakespeare_char, enwik8, and text8.
