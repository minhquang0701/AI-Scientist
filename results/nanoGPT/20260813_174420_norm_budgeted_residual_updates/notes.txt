# Title: Norm-Budgeted Residual Updates for Stable Transformer Dynamics
# Experiment description: Extend GPTConfig with use_norm_budgeted_residual_updates, residual_update_budget, use_tangent_projection, and residual_update_eps. Add helper functions to compute per-token RMS norms and apply a budgeted residual update: optionally remove the component of the attention or MLP update parallel to the current residual stream, then multiply the update by min(1, residual_update_budget * rms(x) / (rms(update) + eps)) before adding it to x. Modify Block.forward only when enabled, applying this helper separately to the attention residual branch and the MLP residual branch; keep the disabled path exactly identical to the baseline. Compare the baseline, soft update-norm budgeting only, tangent projection only, and the combined method across shakespeare_char, enwik8, and text8, with a small sweep over residual_update_budget values such as 0.25, 0.5, and 1.0. Extend logging and estimate_loss to report ordinary train and validation loss, best validation loss, train-val gap, update-to-state RMS ratios by layer, radial versus tangential update fraction, gradient norm, seed variance, total train time, and inference tokens per second. The hypothesis is that constraining residual update geometry improves optimization stability and generalization without changing the data, objective, or parameter count.
