# Title: Temporal-Difference Belief Heads for Predictive-State Language Modeling
# Experiment description: Modify GPT.forward() to optionally return final hidden states and auxiliary belief-head logits for future horizons such as 2, 4, and 8. Keep the ordinary tied lm_head and next-token cross-entropy unchanged as the primary objective. On auxiliary iterations after warmup_iters, run the usual forward pass once, then train each horizon head at position t to match the detached ordinary lm_head distribution at position t + h - 1, since that later state predicts the same token after observing the intervening ground-truth context. Use temperature-scaled KL divergence with T around 2.0, valid only where t + h - 1 is inside the block, and ramp lambda_td from 0.0 to 0.05 or 0.1. Evaluation and generation ignore the auxiliary heads and use the standard lm_head path unchanged. Extend estimate_loss() to report ordinary train/validation loss as the primary metric, TD KL by horizon, horizon-head accuracy diagnostics, train-val gap, total train time, and inference tokens per second. Compare baseline GPT, hard future-token auxiliary heads, TD belief heads, and a shuffled-later-logits control across shakespeare_char, enwik8, and text8.
